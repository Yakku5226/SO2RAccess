### A1. Book count in item creation (Writing / Publication)
- The game DOES have an owned-count slot on that screen: the info panel presenter (`UIItemCreationInformationPresenter`)
  has `itemCount` + `itemCountLabel` fields, and the result-list rows carry `haveCount`. The mod already speaks
  "Have N" for result rows (`ic_have_count`, CampMenuHandler.ItemCreation.cs:615) but ONLY when the hook's
  `dataList` is non-empty. For Writing/Publication every row logs `creation hook (no items): '<book>'` (08:09:33.517,
  08:12:21.778), so the mod returns early (CampMenuHandler.ItemCreation.cs:566-576) and speaks only
  "<book>. Needs Fountain Pen. N of M." (PollActionListFallback, CampMenuHandler.ItemCreation.ActionList.cs:645-686).
- Whether a sighted player sees the count on that exact screen is medium confidence (native body unreadable; the
  mod never logged `isItem`/`itemID`/`itemCount.activeInHierarchy` there). One debug line on the "(no items)" log
  would settle it. Sources for the number: `UIItemCreationInformationData.itemID` or
  `UICampSpecialSkillWritingActionSelector.GetCreateItemID(index)` → `ItemManager.Instance.GetItemCount(itemID)`
  (already used in BattleMenuHandler.ItemSpell.cs:145). Prefer reading the presenter's own `itemCount.text` when visible.
- Size: small once the probe confirms the panel.

### A2. Super specialty: level on Enhance screen, requirements on the IC screen

  screen; the game does not show it there. Optional: shorten "…, met, …, met" to "Requirements met" when both are met.

### A3. World map chest: "path found" vs "cannot be reached" alternating
- Verdict: the chest ("Unopened chest 1" at 949.7, 17.8, −339.0, 54 m from the Lacuer gate) was NEVER physically
  reachable. It sits on a terrace ringed by `Col_Obstacle` CharacterWall fence pieces (L23) with a 1.2–2.3 m step.
  The grid (500 cm climb rule, sub-body gaps between fence pieces) calls it reachable, so the list includes it
  correctly ("never exclude reachable targets"), but no route the grid produces can be walked. The alternation is
  three separate mechanisms, not planner randomness:
  1. **Resume keeps blocked stamps, fresh requests wipe them.** `WorldmapCalculateAndStorePath`
     (NavigationHandler.Worldmap.Pathfinding.cs:466-474) clears `_wmBlockedPositions` unless `keepBlockedPositions`;
     Resume.cs:175 passes true ("kept across battles on purpose"). For "Unopened chest 2" the pre-battle wedge at
     (932.1,−286.7) stamped two blocked discs; with them, A* hugged Lacuer's wall at (934.5,−288) and the body sweep
     refused ("Cannot reach Unopened chest 2", 09:15:10, 09:15:50); without them the fresh plan threaded the same
     wedge point, passed the sweep, and the player wedged at exactly wp 11 every time (09:14:36, 09:15:20, 09:16:22).
     Both verdicts were "right" for their inputs; neither matched physics. High confidence.
  2. **The 25 m "safe exit" leg is never swept on the comfort tier.** `ComputeSafeExitPoint` (Pathfinding.cs:321)
     points away from the nearest map jump; the player stood inside the Lacuer gate ring, so the exit went NE into
     a 2.5 m gap while the chest was SW. The exit leg is swept only on the floor tier (Pathfinding.cs:501-505).
     Every walk that arrived abandoned the exit leg after the wedge (recalc west along z=−291, then south).
  3. **The 16 m endpoint exemption hides the fence.** `WmSweepEndpointExemptDist = 16f` (Pathfinding.cs:56) skips
     sweep segments within 16 m of either end; the fence is ~9 m from chest 1, so long routes are goal-exempt and
     the 13–17 m resume routes are exempt end to end. Stuck-recalcs call `FindPath` directly (Worldmap.cs:405) with
     no sweep. Result: 5 wedge cycles (each a ~0.6 s floor-tier search, ~3 s of stall per attempt, ~30 s of silence)
     then "Cannot reach" (09:18:24, 09:18:54, 09:23:44). The one honest refusal (09:14:26) happened only because
     those wedges were 17–25 m from the exit point.
- Related: two refusal texts for one cause. A sweep refusal sets `LastNoPathWasDisconnected` (Pathfinding.cs:691),
  so a fresh request says "No walkable route … beyond mountains or water" (misleading for a wall 7 m away) while a
  resume says "Cannot reach".
- Related: Resume.cs:192 comment says `_wmRecalcCount` is kept across battles; Pathfinding.cs:470 resets it
  ("attempt 1, 6 blocked zones" at 09:23:21). Comment/code mismatch.
- Related: carry-window misclassification. A real camp-menu open 0.1 s after a battle resume (09:19:51) counted as
  the post-battle blip (`WmResumeCarryWindow = 3f`, Resume.cs:50), so the walk auto-resumed 46 s later when the
  menu closed. Design says a menu open cancels the walk.
- Size: medium to large; needs a design discussion (sweep the exit leg on every tier? shrink or drop the endpoint
  exemption? clear blocked stamps on resume too? unify the refusal wording?). Governing rules: never false arrivals,
  never exclude reachable targets, never weaken obstacle detection without data.
